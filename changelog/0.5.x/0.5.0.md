# 0.5.0 — 2026-11-16

## Added

- **`fbi_get_police_employees`** returns annual sworn officers and civilians by sex, employees per 1,000 people as CDE publishes it, participated population, and coverage at national, state, or agency scope. ([#32](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/32))
- **`include_monthly`** on `fbi_get_arrests`, `fbi_get_hate_crimes`, and `fbi_get_nibrs_breakdown` adds `monthly`: one row per month with counts, rates, population, participated population, and coverage (CDE's `-1` rate is `null`). With it, an all-zero hate-crime or arrest result says whether CDE recognizes the state or ORI, or that its zeros are not reported counts. ([#31](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/31))
- **`fbi_get_arrests` offense `320`** (Suspicion).
- **`fbi-crime-guide` skill**, served as `skill://fbi-crime-guide/SKILL.md` and shipped in `skills/` (npm package, Docker image, `.mcpb` bundle).

## Changed

- **`fbi_get_crime_estimates`** returns the whole requested month window in one call; the output fields `next_from` and `oversize_notice` are removed, and `total_months` counts the window. ([#33](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/33))
- **`fbi_get_arrests`** offense `50` is labeled "Aggravated Assault", and `140` and `150` gain "(Unspecified)"; descriptions say a code counts only its own breakdown row. ([#30](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/30))
- **`fbi_search_agencies` `county`** matches as given first, then without one trailing County, Parish, Borough, Census Area, Municipality, or City and Borough, ignoring case, accents, spaces, periods, apostrophes (including `ʻ` and U+02BC), and hyphens. ([#34](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/34))
- **`fbi_search_agencies` `limit`** above 200 is served at 200 with a notice instead of rejected, and the schema advertises no maximum; `FBI_CLAMP_LIMITS=false` restores the 1–200 schema and rejection. ([#35](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/35))
- **`fbi_get_nibrs_breakdown` `no_data`** hints no longer read all-null values as no reported incidents: the agency hint says CDE also answers months the agency has not submitted with nulls and points to `fbi_get_arrests` with `include_monthly`, and the state and national hints name a window after CDE's latest data month.
- **api.data.gov 403 and 429** refusals carry recovery hints (key refused, request limit).

## Fixed

- **`fbi_get_crime_estimates`** renders numbers in its text in en-US whatever the host locale.
- **A 200 response body that is not JSON** fails once as a serialization error; it was retried four times and reported as a validation error.
- **CDE requests** share one 50-second deadline across attempts and retry waits, so a hung upstream fails with `retry_deadline_exceeded` before an MCP client's 60-second default timeout; `FBI_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS` bounds each attempt within it.

## Removed

- **`FBI_API_BASE_UCR`** setting; no endpoint read it.
