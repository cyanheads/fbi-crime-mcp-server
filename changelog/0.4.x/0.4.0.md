# 0.4.0 — 2026-10-22

## Added

- **`fbi_search_agencies`, `fbi_get_hate_crimes`, `fbi_get_arrests`, and `fbi_get_nibrs_breakdown` are callable again**, back in `tools/list` after 0.3.0 disabled them. Callable tools go from two to six; five retired tools stay disabled. ([#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/21), [#25](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/25))
- **`fbi_search_agencies`** lists a state's agencies from the CDE agency directory with `ori`, type, counties, and NIBRS status, filtered by `name_contains`, `county`, and `agency_type` and paged with `limit` and `offset`. Pass a returned `ori` to agency-scope calls. ([#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/21))
- **`fbi_get_hate_crimes`, `fbi_get_arrests`, and `fbi_get_nibrs_breakdown`** return CDE category totals summed over a month window at national, state, or agency scope, with a `bias` (34 codes) or `offense` (46 arrest codes, 71 NIBRS codes) selector. Years start at 1991 (hate crime, NIBRS) and 1985 (arrests). ([#25](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/25))
- **`include_comparisons`** on `fbi_get_crime_estimates` adds `comparisons`: the state and United States (agency scope) or United States (state scope) rates, populations, and coverage for the scoped months. Default `false`; `true` on national scope fails with `scope_param_incompatible`. ([#24](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/24))

## Changed

- **`fbi_get_crime_estimates` years** accept 1985 through the current year (was 2000–2030). A year after the current UTC year fails with `year_out_of_range` instead of an upstream fetch error. ([#23](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/23))
- **`fbi_get_leoka` years** accept 2020 through the current year. Years 2000–2019 now fail input validation (`invalid_arguments`) instead of returning `no_data`, and later years fail with `year_out_of_range`. The `no_data` recovery states the 2020 start. ([#23](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/23))
- **Blank `state_abbr` and `ori`** strings from form clients read as unset.
- **Rate descriptions** say per 100,000 participating population, the denominator CDE uses. ([#24](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/24))
- **Discovery text** (server instructions, the `ori` description, the `no_data` recovery, and the disabled `fbi_get_agency` reason) points to `fbi_search_agencies`. ([#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/21))

## Fixed

- **`fbi_get_leoka` `officer_death_by_month`** no longer reports CDE zero fill as counts. Monthly responses keep only the requested month under each year, and current-year YTD responses drop months after the current UTC month; genuine zeros and past-year YTD series are unchanged. The heading names the period once. ([#22](https://github.com/cyanheads/fbi-crime-mcp-server/issues/22))
